FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does setup take?
5 minutes. Add one snippet to your app. Events are auto-captured immediately. No manual tagging required (though you can add custom events anytime).
Do I need a developer to set it up?
For basic setup, no. Any product manager can do it. For advanced custom events or API integrations, you might want 30 minutes of dev time.
Can I migrate from Google Analytics / Amplitude / Mixpanel?
Yes. We have a 1-click data import for all three. Your historical data comes with you.
Is my data safe?
Clearview is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and offers EU-hosted data residency. You can also self-host on your own infrastructure (we're open source).
What if I outgrow the free plan?
You'll get a heads-up well before you hit the limit. Upgrading takes one click, and there's no retroactive billing. You only pay for what you use going forward.
