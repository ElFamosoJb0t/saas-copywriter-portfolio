## RÈGLES POUR CHAQUE PROPOSAL

1. **Ligne 1** : Prouve que tu as lu le job post (jamais générique)
2. **Ligne 2-3** : Ce que tu fais + pourquoi c'est pertinent pour EUX
3. **1 lien** vers le sample le plus pertinent (pas 3 liens)
4. **1 question ouverte** à la fin (engage la conversation)
5. **Max 150 mots** — les clients ne lisent pas les pavés
6. **Jamais** : "Dear Sir/Madam", "I am a highly experienced", "I would love the opportunity"
7. **Toujours** : ton prénom, ton site, confiance sans arrogance

## PHRASES D'ACCROCHE (à mixer)

### Quand le job mentionne le trafic/SEO :
- "Ranking on page 1 is step one — the real challenge is making that traffic convert."
- "Most SaaS blogs rank for keywords that attract readers, not buyers. I focus on the second kind."

### Quand le job mentionne les emails :
- "The difference between a 4% and 11% trial-to-paid rate usually isn't the product — it's the onboarding emails."
- "If your welcome email gets a 45% open rate but a 3% click rate, the problem isn't deliverability — it's the copy."

### Quand le job mentionne la conversion :
- "A landing page that 'looks great' but converts at 1.5% is an expensive design project, not a growth asset."
- "Copy converts when it answers the visitor's real question: 'Is this worth my time in the next 30 seconds?'"

### Quand le job est vague :
- "You need content that works as hard as your product team. I can help with that."
- "Most SaaS content checks a box. The good stuff moves a metric. I write the second kind."
