# Welcome Email Sequence — SaaS Project Management Tool
*Email Sequence Sample — 5 emails — Onboarding / Activation*

---

## Sequence Strategy

**Goal**: Guide new free trial users to activation (create project → invite team → complete first task) within 7 days.

**Trigger**: User signs up for free trial.

**Timing**: Behavioral triggers + time-based fallbacks.

**Tone**: Helpful, concise, peer-to-peer (not corporate).
